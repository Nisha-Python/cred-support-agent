# Credit Score Impact Factors

A credit score can be affected by repayment history, credit utilization, outstanding debt, the age and mix of credit accounts, and recent credit applications. Consistently making repayments on time can support a healthy credit history.

Missing payments or carrying excessive outstanding balances may negatively affect creditworthiness. Multiple credit applications within a short period may also influence a credit profile.

A support agent should explain general credit-score factors without claiming to know an individual's exact score impact unless verified information is available.
