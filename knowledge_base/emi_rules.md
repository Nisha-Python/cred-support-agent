# EMI Calculation Rules

Equated Monthly Instalment (EMI) is the regular amount paid toward a loan each month. EMI depends mainly on the principal amount, annual interest rate, and loan tenure.

For a standard reducing-balance loan, EMI can be calculated using the principal, monthly interest rate, and number of monthly instalments. A longer tenure generally reduces the monthly EMI but can increase the total interest paid over the life of the loan.

The EMI schedule should clearly separate the interest and principal components so that the borrower can understand how the outstanding balance changes over time.
