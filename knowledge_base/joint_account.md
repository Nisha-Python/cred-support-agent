# Joint Account Rules

A joint account is held by more than one account holder, and the operating rules depend on the mandate selected when the account is opened. The mandate determines how transactions or instructions can be authorized.

Changes to a joint account may require verification or consent from one or more account holders depending on the requested action and the account mandate. Support staff should verify the applicable account instructions before confirming what a particular holder can do.

Sensitive information belonging to one account holder should not be disclosed to another person without appropriate authorization.
