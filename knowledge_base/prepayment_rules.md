# Prepayment and Penalty Rules

Prepayment allows a borrower to repay some or all of the outstanding loan before the scheduled maturity date. Depending on the loan product and applicable policy, a prepayment charge may apply.

The applicable charge can depend on factors such as the loan type, repayment stage, amount being prepaid, and terms accepted when the loan was issued. Customers should check the applicable loan agreement and current policy before making a prepayment.

A support agent should not promise that a penalty is waived unless the available policy explicitly supports that conclusion.
