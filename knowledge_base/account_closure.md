# Account Closure Process

An account-closure request should first be verified to ensure that the request is authorized by the account holder. Before closure, outstanding dues, pending transactions, linked services, and other unresolved obligations should be checked.

Any remaining balance should be handled according to the applicable account-closure procedure. Once all required checks are complete, the account can be marked for closure and the customer can receive confirmation.

Records required for regulatory, audit, or dispute purposes should be retained according to the applicable retention requirements even after an account is closed.
