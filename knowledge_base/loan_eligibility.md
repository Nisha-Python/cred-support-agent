# Loan Eligibility Criteria

Eligibility depends on the loan category, applicant profile, repayment capacity, and required documentation. Personal Loan applicants generally need stable income and an acceptable credit history, while Home Loan applicants are evaluated using income, property details, repayment capacity, and credit history.

Auto Loan eligibility considers income, credit history, and the value of the vehicle being financed. Education Loan eligibility may depend on the student's admission to an eligible institution, course details, co-applicant information, and repayment capability.

Business Loan applicants may need to provide business financial information, operating history, and evidence that the proposed borrowing can be serviced from business cash flows.
