# Minimum Balance Requirements

Some deposit accounts require customers to maintain a specified minimum balance. The required balance can differ according to the account type and applicable product rules.

If the balance falls below the required level, a charge may apply when the account terms permit such a charge. Customers should be informed about the relevant account-specific requirement rather than being given a universal minimum balance.

Balance requirements and related charges should be checked against the latest applicable product policy before providing a definitive answer.
