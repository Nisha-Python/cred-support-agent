# KYC Document Requirements

Know Your Customer (KYC) verification is used to establish and verify the identity and address of an applicant. Common KYC evidence can include government-issued identity documents, address proof, and other documents required by the applicable verification process.

Applicants should provide documents that are valid, readable, and consistent with the information submitted in the application. Additional verification may be requested when the submitted information cannot be adequately verified.

Sensitive identification numbers should be handled securely and should not be unnecessarily exposed in application logs, support messages, or demonstrations.
