# Credit Card Fee Structure

Credit-card charges can include annual or renewal fees, late-payment charges, cash-advance fees, foreign-transaction charges, and applicable taxes. The exact fee depends on the card product and the transaction type.

Customers should review the applicable fee schedule before using a service that may incur a charge. Fees and taxes should be disclosed clearly so that customers can understand the total amount payable.

Promotional fee waivers may have eligibility conditions and should not be assumed to apply to every customer or transaction.
