# NRI Account Eligibility

Non-Resident Indians (NRIs) may be eligible for specific banking products designed for customers residing outside India. Eligibility and permitted account types depend on the customer's residential status, applicable regulations, and product rules.

An NRI applicant may need to provide identity, address, residential-status, and other required documents during onboarding or verification. The exact documentation can vary according to the product and the customer's circumstances.

Support staff should verify the applicable NRI product policy before confirming eligibility or permitted transactions for an individual customer.
