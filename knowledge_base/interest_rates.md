# Interest Rate Slabs

Loan interest rates may vary according to factors such as loan category, applicant risk profile, credit history, loan amount, and repayment tenure. A rate slab groups eligible applications into predefined interest-rate ranges.

For example, a lending policy may assign lower rates to stronger credit profiles and higher rates to profiles with greater assessed risk. The actual rate applied to an application should come from the applicable policy and not from an unsupported assumption.

Support responses should explain the relevant rate rule and should avoid presenting an illustrative rate as a guaranteed customer-specific offer.
