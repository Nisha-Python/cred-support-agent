# Fraud and Dispute Resolution Process

A customer who notices an unauthorized transaction should report it through the appropriate support channel as soon as possible. The support team can record the dispute, review the relevant transaction information, and initiate the applicable investigation process.

The investigation may require transaction details and additional verification from the customer. A fraud-review flag indicates that additional investigation may be necessary and does not by itself prove that fraud occurred.

The final resolution should be based on the investigation results and applicable banking policies, with the customer receiving an appropriate status update.
